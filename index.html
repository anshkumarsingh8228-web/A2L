About Alone2Lone

A friendship-first place to connect, chat and play.

Why Alone2Lone?

The idea is simple: turn “alone” into your own circle of people.

🤝 Friendship-first

Meet through shared interests.

🛡️ Safety-focused

Blocking, reporting and privacy controls belong at the core.

🎮 Play together

Games can make starting a conversation easier.

Terms & Conditions

Prototype terms.

1. Respect others

Harassment, threats, spam and abusive behaviour are not allowed.

2. Protect private information

Do not share passwords, phone numbers, addresses or sensitive information.

3. Games

Use games responsibly and respectfully.

4. Prototype notice

This single-file demo uses browser-local storage and cross-tab events for prototype interactions; it has no production real-time backend, accounts or payment processing.

Privacy Policy

How this prototype handles information.

Local storage

Demo chat data is stored locally in your browser.

Phone protection

Messages containing phone-number-like 10-digit sequences are rejected, and displayed phone digits are masked.

No real account system

This prototype does not send your demo profile or chats to a server.

Production version

A live service should use secure authentication, encrypted transport, proper database security, moderation and a complete legal privacy policy.
